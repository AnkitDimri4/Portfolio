Prices, payments and refunds

  • Your first PDF is ₹1. After that, each PDF is what you choose to pay, from ₹10.
  • Plans: ₹49 for 6 months, or ₹99 for a year, covers every PDF you email to that address and keeps your resumes and drafts until it ends. You pay once: a plan doesn't renew by itself.
  • Prices are in Indian rupees, and nothing is added at checkout. You pay on the page the PDF's email links to, before you download it.
  • Payments go through Razorpay, by UPI, card or net banking. I never see your card or bank details.
  • Until that checkout opens on the site, you pay by UPI: to my UPI ID, or its QR code, on the download page, then you enter the payment's 12-digit reference. Your first PDF (₹1) unlocks at once; for later PDFs and plans, I check the payment in my UPI app and unlock it, usually within a few hours, and you get an email either way.
  • Delivery: it's all online. The PDF unlocks as soon as the payment goes through (by UPI, at once for your first PDF, otherwise once I've confirmed it), and a plan starts then: there's nothing to ship.
  • Order ID: every payment has one. The download page shows it, and you get an email with it whenever your payment goes through, fails or is refunded. Quote it if you write to me about a payment.
  • Refunds: if you paid and the PDF didn't unlock, or you paid twice by mistake, email me within 7 days with the order ID and I'll refund it to the way you paid: a UPI payment back by UPI, and through Razorpay, your bank takes 5 to 7 working days to return it. You get an email when it's refunded. Otherwise payments aren't refundable, since what you pay for is delivered at once.
